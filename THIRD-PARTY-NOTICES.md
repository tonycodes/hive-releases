The Arc v0.1.0 desktop build is primarily built with Rust, Tauri, Tree-sitter,
SQLite, libgit2, OpenSSL, and related crates from crates.io. The dependency
license inventory for the current build includes licenses and license
expressions such as:

- MIT
- Apache-2.0
- BSD-2-Clause and BSD-3-Clause

- Zlib
- 0BSD

- MPL-2.0
- CC0-1.0
- CDLA-Permissive-2.0

Some dependency license expressions offer a choice of licenses. Where a choice
is available, Arc relies on a permissive option such as MIT or Apache-2.0
unless a component's own notice states otherwise.

Third-party source packages and their license files are available from their
upstream package registries and repositories, including crates.io. If you
believe a required third-party notice is missing or incomplete, please report it
through the support channel listed in the public repository.
